What We Believe

Our values, squeezed into everything we do.

Radical Freshness

We press, we don't pour. Every fruit is cut that morning. Every cup is made in front of you. Freshness isn't a feature — it's the only way we know how to work.

Community First

We source from local farms, hire from the neighborhood, and price our menu so everyone can walk in. Citrus belongs to the community that made it possible.

Personality Over Polish

We're named after a cat. That tells you everything. We'd rather be genuine and a little weird than polished and forgettable. The cat gets a line on every menu.

Obsessive Craft

We test recipes until the balance is exactly right. Not good enough — exactly right. That obsession is what separates a drink worth remembering from one you finish and forget.

Sustainable Heart

Recyclable packaging, zero waste practices, and a menu that evolves with the season. We take care of the ingredients. The ingredients take care of you.

Warmth Without Pretension

No reservation required to feel welcome here. Every customer — first-timer or hundredth-timer — gets the same warmth, the same care, the same smile.

Our Journey

From one cat to one community.

🐱

Early 2024

A Cat Named Citrus

A small orange tabby cat inspires a name. Her owner starts experimenting with cold-pressed citrus drinks at home, sharing samples with neighbors. They keep asking for more.

🍋

Mid 2024

The First Recipe

After 47 test batches, the Citrus Classic Lemonade is born — cold-pressed lemon, honey syrup, salt rim. The recipe that would define everything that followed.

🏠

Late 2024

Opening Day in Imus

Citrus quietly opens its doors in Imus, Cavite. No grand launch, no investors — just a blender, a lot of lemons, and genuine belief. The neighborhood shows up. And keeps showing up.

⭐

Today

1,000+ Guests & Growing

Over a thousand guests served, a full menu of citrus-inspired food and drink, and a community that has made this place their own. The cat still gets full credit.
