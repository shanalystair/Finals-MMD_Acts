Our Story

It started with a cat named Citrus.

It wasn't a business plan — it was a nickname. A small, citrus-orange tabby cat who always looked like she had somewhere important to be.

In 2024, Citrus quietly opened its doors. No grand launch. No investors. Just a blender, a lot of lemons, and a belief that if you make something with genuine love, people will find it.

Today, Citrus is a neighborhood spot where students study, couples share cold drinks, and regulars become friends.
